What is the Volume Shader BM Benchmark?

The Volume Shader BM Benchmark is an established browser-based graphics test created to push modern GPU architectures to their computational limits. Unlike conventional polygon-based 3D benchmarks that evaluate triangle rasterization speeds and geometry pipelines, Volume Shader BM computes dynamic volumetric fluid, plasma, and absorption math directly per-pixel inside the fragment shader stage.

By traversing 3D space with iterative raymarching, the shader samples volumetric noise at dozens or hundreds of discrete intervals per screen pixel. This makes it an exceptional tool for assessing GPU thermal throttling, hardware power delivery, and driver compilation efficiency across mobile, laptop, and desktop devices.

How Volumetric Raymarching Stresses Graphics Hardware

1. Ray Traversal

A ray vector is cast from the virtual viewport into dynamic 3D space per screen pixel.

2. Fractal Noise

Continuous mathematical noise functions evaluate volumetric density at each ray step.

3. Light Scattering

The Beer-Lambert equation integrates density and color to simulate realistic light transmission.

Because this process requires thousands of high-precision floating-point arithmetic operations (ALU operations) per frame, it acts as an intense synthetic workload without demanding hundreds of megabytes of texture downloads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this benchmark safe to execute on my device?

Yes. The test executes in your web browser's secure WebGL sandbox. It cannot modify voltages or bypass internal hardware thermal safeguards.

Why is my FPS locked at 60 on a 144Hz monitor?

Browsers often enforce V-Sync by default. You can unlock higher frame rates by disabling vertical sync in your graphics driver control panel or launching your browser with the --disable-frame-rate-limit flag.

What does the ANGLE renderer mean?

ANGLE (Almost Native Graphics Layer Engine) is a Google Chromium layer that translates WebGL calls to Direct3D (Windows) or Metal (macOS) for peak compatibility.
